# Resources — Calculus

---

## Books

- [Calculus 7E](https://github.com/rohanmistry231/Mathematics-for-Machine-Learning-Books/blob/main/Calculus.pdf) 

  A free book written specifically for Calculus ML. Cover everything you need to get started with calculus 1 and 2.

---

## Courses

- [Math for ML and Data Science Specialization](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/mathematics-for-machine-learning-and-data-science) — Course 2 · Coursera  
  Taught by DeepLearning.AI. The second course focuses entirely on calculus for ML: from limits to chain rule with a practical, hands-on approach using Python. The best structured starting point.

---

## Free / Online

- [Essence of calculus](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLZHQObOWTQDPD3MizzM2xVFitgF8hE_ab) — 3Blue1Brown  
  A visual, intuition-first YouTube series by Grant Sanderson. Doesn't teach computation — teaches you what calculus *means* geometrically. Watch this before or alongside anything else.

- [Calculus](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/calculus) — Khan Academy  
  Free, beginner-friendly, and well-paced. Good for building comfort with the mechanics like limits, continuity and more, if you need to go slow and practice.
