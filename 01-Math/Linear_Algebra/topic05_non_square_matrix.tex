\subsection*{Summary}
A non-square matrix (e.g.\ $2\times3$) transforms vectors between spaces of different dimensions.

\subsection{Non-Square Matrix}

Defined as a \textbf{$2\times3$ matrix}, where the 3 columns are the basis vectors $\hat{i}$, $\hat{j}$, and $\hat{k}$ --- meaning we are in \textbf{three-dimensional} space, and the two rows mean the output is in \textbf{two-dimensional} space.

\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item 3 columns $\rightarrow$ input is 3D
  \item 2 rows $\rightarrow$ output is 2D
  \item The landing spots mean the \textbf{output is in two-dimensional space}
\end{itemize}
